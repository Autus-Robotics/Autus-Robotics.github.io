Our services

Retail and logistics automation with a physical result: fuller, more accurate shelves — proven in a short pilot before you commit.

🔍

Shelf scanning & on-shelf availability

A robot cell scans your aisles and flags gaps, misplaced products and pricing errors. You get an accurate, continuously updated picture of what’s actually on the shelf — the foundation for reducing out-of-stocks without adding staff hours.

🤖

Robotic restocking, picking & palletising

The same perception that finds the gaps can drive a robot to act on them — restocking shelves, or picking and palletising in a warehouse. We build on standard, proven robots (for example Universal Robots) with our own perception and control software, so the cell fits your environment.

📱

Photo→3-D planogram digital twins

Our perception software turns an ordinary shelf photo into a textured 3-D product/tray model — a live planogram digital twin. Retailers use it to keep planograms accurate without manual measuring, and the same model can drive a robot to keep the shelf full.

🎯

Fixed-fee pilots that de-risk automation

We start small. A short, fixed-fee pilot on one aisle or one line, with a success measure (KPI) agreed up front and a clear “if we hit it, here’s what comes next” path. The pilot fee is credited toward a wider rollout — so you find out cheaply whether it works before any large investment. Deployment is available as a one-off or as a monthly robot-as-a-service.
