About Autus Robotics

A Berlin robotics company making retail and logistics work fuller, faster and more accurate.

Autus Robotics builds and operates robot cells that automate physical tasks in retail and logistics — starting with scanning and restocking supermarket shelves, and extending to picking and palletising. Our perception software also turns an ordinary shelf photo into a 3-D product/tray digital twin, so retailers get a live planogram model as well as a robot that can act on it.

We combine standard, proven robots (for example Universal Robots) with our own perception and control software, and we prove value in a short, fixed-fee pilot before any large commitment. The result is automation that works in the real world — on a real aisle or line — not just in a slide deck.

What we stand for

Our approach

Outcomes, not buzzwords

We sell a measurable result — on-shelf availability, labour hours saved, fewer stockouts — and agree the success measure before the pilot starts.

Proven hardware, our software

Reliable off-the-shelf robots plus perception and control software we build in-house. Less bespoke risk, faster to deploy, easier to maintain.

Start small, de-risked

A fixed-fee pilot on one aisle or line, credited toward deployment. You find out cheaply whether it works before committing.
